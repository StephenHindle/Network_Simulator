Help and Shortcuts

Canvas Gestures

  • Scroll wheel zooms in and out toward the cursor
  • Space + drag or middle mouse drag pans the canvas
  • Drag devices to move them, double-click to open settings
  • Click a cable to select it, double-click to delete it
  • Click a port label (Fa0/1 and similar) to shut down or enable that interface
  • Use the minimap in the bottom right to jump around large networks

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Delete / Backspace removes the selected device, label or cable
  • Escape cancels connect mode or deselects everything
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo
  • Ctrl+D duplicates the selected device
  • Ctrl+S saves the topology to a file

Building a Network

  • Drag devices from the sidebar flyouts onto the canvas
  • Use Connect mode to cable devices together, choosing ports where prompted
  • Laptops connect wirelessly (set the SSID and password in their settings to match an AP) and can also be cabled to a switch
  • Switches support VLANs, trunking and STP; routers run RIPv2 with static route support
  • Devices have Cisco style CLIs with tab completion, opened from their settings panel
  • Use the Ping Tool, Packet Sniffer and packet animations to trace traffic
  • Work is autosaved to your browser every 30 seconds and offered back on reload
